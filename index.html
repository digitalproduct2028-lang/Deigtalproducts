    Is this a physical book?

    No. You get a printable PDF file. Nothing is shipped, so you can start the same day.

    How do I get the file after paying?

    After payment on SuperProfile, the download link is shown on screen and sent to your email. Check your Spam or Promotions folder if you don’t see it.

    Can I print the pages many times?

    Yes. Print as often as you like for your own children or your own classroom. Please don’t share or resell the file.

    What age is it for?

    Each product shows its age range. Our books cover ages 2 to 10. The ADHD worksheets come in 5 age-based Parts, so you can start at your child’s age.

    Can I get a refund?

    Because the files are digital, purchases can’t be refunded after download. If a file is broken or you got the wrong one, contact us and we’ll fix it.
